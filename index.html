Our Story

From Our Little Kitchen
To Your Sweetest Moments

Sweet Crumbs began in 2014 as a single home oven in Gulberg, Lahore, baking birthday cakes for neighbours. A decade later, we still measure success the same way — one happy bite at a time. Every recipe is tested, tasted and tweaked by hand before it ever reaches a display case.

No preservatives, no shortcuts, no rushed mornings. Just real butter, real fruit, and a team that genuinely loves what leaves this kitchen.
